Sawyer & Sons — 1961 No. 0041

How we got the room

Sixty-one years of other people’s keys

Sawyer & Sons cut keys at this address until the last Sawyer retired and left the back room exactly as it stood — pegboard, ledger, and a floor safe nobody has opened since.

We changed three things: the lighting, the ice, and the hours. The pegboard stays.
